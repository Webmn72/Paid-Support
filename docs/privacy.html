Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This policy describes how the Paid iOS app ("Paid", "we", "us") handles information. Paid is a bill and pay-period planning app. It helps you plan from the information you enter, import, scan, or confirm. Paid does not connect to your bank, does not move money, and does not make payments for you.

Information Paid stores

The Paid app stores the planning data you create or confirm, which may include account names, bill names, bill amounts, due dates, paid status, income schedules, planned and actual pay amounts, missed-pay status, received dates, categories, Everyday Spending settings, planning settings, balances you enter, notes, shared-account details, and backup/import data.

Paid stores this data:

  • Locally on your device, using Apple's built-in app storage; and
  • Optionally in your personal iCloud account through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services, if you use iCloud Sync.

Paid does not operate its own server for your planning data. We do not receive a copy of your Paid data unless you choose to send information to us, such as by emailing support or sharing a diagnostic export.

Information we do not sell or use for advertising

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial information with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or ad networks. Paid does not include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics trackers.

No bank connections

Paid does not connect to banks, credit unions, credit cards, or other financial institutions. We do not access bank credentials, account balances, or transaction feeds from third parties. Planning values in Paid are based on the information you enter, import, scan, or confirm.

iCloud Sync and sharing

If you use iCloud Sync, Paid stores and syncs your data through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services so it can appear on your devices. Apple processes that data under Apple's iCloud terms, security, and privacy practices.

If you share an account with another person, the shared account data is accessible to invited collaborators after they accept the invitation. Collaborators may retain access to shared data according to the sharing state and their own device/iCloud copies. Removing Paid from your device does not automatically remove another collaborator's copy or end all sharing access.

Camera, photos, PDFs, and spreadsheet imports

Paid Pro includes optional bill scanning and import features. When you choose to scan a bill, select a photo, select a PDF, or import a spreadsheet, Paid processes that content on device using Apple platform capabilities and the app's import logic. Imported or scanned bill information is only saved into Paid after you review and confirm it.

Paid does not upload camera images, selected photos, PDFs, or spreadsheets to a server operated by us.

Notifications

Paid can send optional bill reminders and cross-device bill-paid alerts. Notification content may include bill names, amounts, due dates, or paid-status information. Notification permission belongs to each device. Paid asks only when a reminder-enabled bill or cross-device paid alerts need permission, including once on another device when reminder-enabled bills sync there. Notifications are handled through Apple's notification system and are not sent through our servers.

Backups and device backups

Paid lets you export user-created backup files. A backup file may contain account details, bills, income schedules and actuals, categories, payments, notes, balances, and planning amounts. You choose where to save or share backup files, and you are responsible for storing them somewhere private.

Paid also stores local app data on your device. Depending on your device backup settings, iOS may include local Paid data in a device backup.

Reports export

Paid Pro can export a report as CSV when you ask for it. Paid writes the selected account and date-range summary and the bill rows included by your report filters to a temporary file, then you choose where to save or share it. Paid does not send the report automatically.

Purchases

Paid Pro purchases and restore-purchase checks are processed by Apple using StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card number. Apple may provide transaction and entitlement information needed for the app to unlock Paid Pro features.

TestFlight diagnostics and support

In Debug builds, Paid may keep detailed developer logs for local development. In verified TestFlight builds, Paid may keep and export a limited allowlist of redacted diagnostic events. These event logs are limited to event names, categories, counts, and non-user error codes. They are designed to exclude names, amounts, balances, identifiers, dates, URLs, tokens, imported content, and raw error descriptions.

TestFlight Developer Tools may also let you create a separate Budget Rollover report when you ask for one. This detailed report can include account and bill names, budget amounts, balances, dates, identifiers, and payment totals from the active account. Paid warns you about that content before you choose where to save or share the report. Paid does not send or share the report automatically.

App Store production builds do not enable in-app diagnostic collection, viewing, or export. If you contact support by email or choose to share a diagnostic report, we receive only the information you decide to send.

Children

Paid is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Paid keeps your app data until you delete it, erase an account, remove local app data, remove iCloud data, or remove shared-account access. Because Paid does not operate its own server for your planning data, there is usually no server-side copy for us to delete.

To remove Paid data:

  • Use Paid's in-app erase or account deletion features for local and owned-iCloud deletion paths.
  • Delete the app from a device to remove local app data from that device.
  • To remove iCloud data outside the app, use Apple's iCloud storage controls, such as Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, then remove Paid data where available.
  • For shared accounts, the account owner and collaborators may each need to manage their own access, local copies, and sharing state.

Apple services

Paid relies on Apple platform services, including local app storage, iCloud/CloudKit, StoreKit, UserNotifications, camera/photo/document picker features, PDF processing, and TestFlight for beta distribution. Apple's handling of information through these services is governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, make a note in the app or on this support site.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please email paidappsupport@icloud.com.
