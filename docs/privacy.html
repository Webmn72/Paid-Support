Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

This policy describes how the Paid iOS app ("Paid", "we", "us") handles information. Paid is designed to keep you in control of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account.

Information we collect

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal or financial information. Paid does not include analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or third-party data brokers.

All of the information you enter into Paid — including income, bills, categories, balances, and pay-period settings — is stored:

  • Locally on your device, using Apple's built-in Core Data storage; and
  • Optionally in your personal iCloud account, if you have iCloud enabled for Paid.

Your iCloud data is transmitted and stored by Apple under Apple's iCloud security standards. We never receive a copy of your Paid data on any server of ours.

iCloud sync and account sharing

If you enable iCloud for Paid, your data is synced across your devices that are signed in to the same Apple ID. If you share an account with another person (for example, a partner or family member), the data you choose to share is accessible to that person on their device through Apple's CloudKit sharing. You can stop sharing at any time from the app.

No bank connections

Paid does not connect to banks, credit unions, credit cards, or any financial institution. We do not access account credentials, balances, or transactions from third parties. All information is entered manually by you.

Notifications

Paid can send optional local notifications (for example, bill reminders). These are generated and scheduled on your device and are not sent through our servers.

Children

Paid is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Because Paid does not transmit your data to us, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove your data:

  • Delete the app from your device to remove its local data.
  • To remove iCloud data, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Paid, and choose to delete the app's iCloud data.

Third-party services

Paid relies on Apple platform services only — specifically Core Data and iCloud/CloudKit for storage and sync. No other third-party services receive your data.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, make a note in the app or on this support site.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please email paidappsupport@icloud.com.
