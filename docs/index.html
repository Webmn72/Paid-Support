Frequently asked questions

How does Paid work?
Paid helps you plan your money around your pay periods. It shows what is needed for bills, what is set aside for your plan, and what is safe to spend today — without tracking every transaction or connecting to your bank.
What can I use for free?
Free users can use one personal iCloud-synced account, add bills and income manually, view bills in a list or calendar, use basic reminders, see the current pay-period Budget, view Safe Balance Today, and export backups.
What does Paid Pro unlock?
Paid Pro is a one-time unlock for advanced features: future pay periods, Plan Recovery, advanced planning controls, multiple-account creation, sharing invitations, spreadsheet import, bill scanning, and advanced reports.
Does Paid connect to my bank?
No. Paid never connects to your bank. You enter your income and bills yourself, which keeps you in full control and means Paid never sees your account credentials.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Your data is stored on your device using Apple's built-in storage. If you enable iCloud, your data is synced securely across your devices through Apple's iCloud service and is end-to-end encrypted according to Apple's iCloud security standards.
Can I share my data with others?
Yes. Paid supports sharing an account with another person — for example, a partner or family member — so you can manage finances together over iCloud. Creating a new shared-account invitation requires Paid Pro. People you invite can accept and use one shared account without buying Pro first.
What happens to my data if I delete the app?
Local data is removed with the app. If you had iCloud enabled, your data remains in your iCloud account and will return if you reinstall and sign in with the same Apple ID. You can also delete iCloud data from the iOS Settings app under your Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
How much does Paid cost?
Paid is a free download with an optional one-time Paid Pro unlock. Please see the current App Store listing for local pricing. Paid does not show ads and does not sell your data.

Troubleshooting

If something isn't working as expected, try these first:

  • Make sure Paid is updated to the latest version in the App Store.
  • Force-quit and reopen the app.
  • If you use sync, check Settings → [your name] → iCloud and confirm Paid is turned on.
  • Check your internet connection if shared data is not appearing.
  • Restart your device if the issue persists.
  • If it still isn't working, email us at paidappsupport@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and a short description.

Privacy
