Frequently asked questions

How does Paid work?
Paid helps you plan your money around your pay periods. It shows what is needed for bills, what is set aside for your plan, and what is safe to spend today — without tracking every transaction or connecting to your bank.
What can I use for free?
Free users can use one personal iCloud Sync account, add bills and fixed, variable, or one-time income manually, record actual or missed pay, view bills in a list or calendar, use per-bill reminders, see the current pay-period Budget and Safe Balance Today, review Reports for the last two months, and export backups.
What does Paid Pro unlock?
Paid Pro is a one-time unlock for future pay periods, variable-income insights, Money for Later, Fix My Plan, advanced planning controls, multiple-account creation, sharing invitations, spreadsheet import, bill scanning, longer and filtered Reports, trends, bill details, and CSV report export.
How do income dates and Reports work?
The scheduled payday decides which Budget pay period owns the income. A saved actual replaces the Plan With amount for that occurrence, and a missed pay contributes zero. In Reports, an actual uses Date received for date-range inclusion when you supplied one; this can move the deposit between report ranges without moving its Budget pay period. Report Difference is income minus bills for the selected range, not money that is safe to spend.
Why did another device ask for notification permission?
Notification permission belongs to each device. If a bill with Reminder turned on syncs to another device, Paid may ask once there so that device can schedule the reminder. Cross-Device Bill Paid Alerts can also notify your other devices or invited account participants when a bill is marked paid elsewhere, without alerting the device that made the change.
Does Paid connect to my bank?
No. Paid never connects to your bank. You enter your income and bills yourself, which keeps you in full control and means Paid never sees your account credentials.
What is Assume Paid?
Assume Paid is an optional bill setting. When it is on, Paid records the bill as paid in full on the day after its due date. Paid does not check your bank or verify that the payment cleared, and you can mark the bill unpaid again if needed.
Can changing a future bill alter my current pay period?
On day one of a new pay period, Paid settles the current plan using the information available then, so a future bill change can affect what the current period commits forward. From day two onward, a change that applies only to a future period updates future periods but does not rewrite the current period's committed amount. Current-period payments and an intentional Balance Today update can still change today's figures.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Your data is stored on your device using Apple's built-in storage. If you use iCloud Sync, your data is stored and synced through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services under Apple's terms, security, and privacy practices. Paid does not operate its own server for your planning data.
Can I share my data with others?
Yes. Paid supports sharing an account with another person — for example, a partner or family member — through iCloud Sync. Creating a new shared-account invitation requires Paid Pro. People you invite can accept and use one shared account without buying Pro first.
What happens to my data if I delete the app?
Deleting the app removes local app data from that device. If you use iCloud Sync, synced data may remain in iCloud until you remove it through Paid's erase/account deletion features or Apple's iCloud storage controls. Shared accounts may also require the owner and collaborators to manage their own access and copies.
How much does Paid cost?
Paid is a free download with an optional one-time Paid Pro unlock. Please see the current App Store listing for local pricing. Paid does not show ads and does not sell your data.

Troubleshooting

If something isn't working as expected, try these first:

  • Make sure Paid is updated to the latest version in the App Store.
  • Force-quit and reopen the app.
  • If you use sync, check Settings → [your name] → iCloud and confirm Paid is turned on.
  • Check your internet connection if shared data is not appearing.
  • Restart your device if the issue persists.
  • If it still isn't working, email us at paidappsupport@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and a short description.

Privacy

Paid does not sell your personal financial data. Paid stores your planning data on your device and, if you use iCloud Sync, in your iCloud account through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services.
